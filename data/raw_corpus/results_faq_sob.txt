Title: Results FAQ - School of Business Student Guide June 2020

1. I failed a Semester 1 module(s), what do I need to do?
Any student who failed, or did not attempt, a Semester 1 module(s) must now attempt the repeat assessment.
This will not be an exam but a repeat contingency assessment set by your lecturer.
You must register with the Exams Office using the link provided on the Exams page of the NCI website.
You will have to pay a repeat fee and Exams will confirm what this fee is once your student record has been checked.
The repeat contingency assessment details will be released by faculty on Moodle over the course of the week commencing the 22nd June 2020.
It is important therefore that you keep an eye on your Moodle page and check it regularly.

2. I am a student who sought and was granted an Assessment Deferral in Semester 2, what do I need to do?
Any student who applied for and was granted an assessment deferral in semester 2 will complete the repeat contingency assessment
for the relevant module unless advised otherwise by the School Office or lecturer. If you have completed continuous assessments
and received marks for these, those marks will be carried forward. There is no fee to pay as you have been granted a deferral.

3. I am a student who sought and was granted a module deferral in Semester 2, what do I need to do?
A module deferral would normally result in a student returning the following academic year to repeat the module.
You will be allowed to carry any fully completed continuous assessment marks you have received to date.
If you wish to attempt the repeat contingency assessment this academic year, you must register with the Exams Office and
notify your programme coordinator. There is no fee payable in either case.

4. I have failed a module(s) in Semester 2, what should I do now?
You must attempt the repeat contingency assessment. If you have completed continuous assessments attached to this module(s)
and received marks of 40% or more, those marks will be carried forward. There is no fee to pay, but you must register with the
Exams Office using the link provided on the Exams page of the NCI website.

5. I am a student who failed a module in both Semester 1 and Semester 2. What do I need to do now?
You must attempt the repeat contingency assessment for both modules. There will be no exam for either repeat module.
A repeat fee is required for the semester 1 failed module but not for the semester 2 failed module.

6. I feel I have been detrimentally impacted by the Covid-19 restrictions in place over the course of Semester 2.
Can I attempt the repeat contingency assessment?
Yes. Any student who feels they have been detrimentally impacted by the Covid-19 restrictions in Semester 2 can attempt
the repeat contingency assessment. As this will be deemed a first sitting, the marks obtained for any previous attempts will
be set aside. In the unlikely circumstance where a student achieves a lower mark than their previous attempt, they may
apply for an exam review.

7. What do I need to do if I wish to attempt the repeat contingency assessment due to Covid-19 restrictions?
You must register with the Exams Office using the link provided on the Exams page of the NCI website. There is no fee payable.

8. I am confused by all the information, I'm not sure what this means for me. Where should I go with my questions?
If you have questions about your results and what that means for you, contact your programme co-ordinator by email.

9. What is the deadline to register for the repeat contingency assessment?
The deadline for registration is the 8th July 2020. This is not the deadline for payment of repeat fees so you can register
your intention for the repeat contingency assessment and pay any repeat fee at a later date.
